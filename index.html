i About

I like solving problems that don't have a ready-made answer. Most of the things I build start with something that takes too long, a process that feels unnecessarily complicated, scattered information, or an idea that needs the right system behind it.

I'm a software engineer who enjoys understanding how people actually work, breaking problems down, and turning them into practical digital solutions, whether that means a website, custom application, internal tool, automated workflow, or a complete business system. My background in Information Technology gave me a broad foundation in software, web development, databases, and system design, while real projects taught me that technology is only valuable when it solves the right problem.

I approach every project based on what it actually needs, choosing the right tools and building around the people who will use it. From the first idea to the final product, I enjoy figuring things out, building them, and making them better. Give me a problem, and I'll work toward a solution that actually works.

v Engagements

Three ways to bring me in, depending on where you're starting from.

Build something new

Start with an idea. End with a working system. From business websites to custom applications and internal tools, I take an idea from the initial concept through design, development, deployment, and everything needed to make it usable in the real world.

Improve what already exists

Have something that works, but not quite right? I can step into an existing website, application, or business system, understand how it works, and improve the parts that are slowing you down, causing problems, or holding the business back.

Solve a specific problem

Sometimes you don't need a whole new system. A manual process, disconnected data, repetitive task, or business problem may only need the right solution. I can help identify what's getting in the way and build the tools, automation, or workflow needed to fix it.
